Norwin's Homecoming Week is a time for students, alumni, families and community members to reconnect around traditions that span generations. During this year's celebration, the Norwin School District Community Foundation is also marking another annual tradition: the 2026 Days of Giving.

Taking place October 5-10, the Days of Giving gives members of the Norwin community an opportunity to support programs that directly benefit students and educators throughout the district.

This year's campaign will focus on several areas of need. The Foundation is working to strengthen some of its smaller endowed scholarships so they can continue providing meaningful awards to graduating Norwin students. Donations will also support Educator Innovation Grants, which provide funding for new classroom projects and learning opportunities proposed by Norwin educators, as well as the district's A+ Facilities initiative.

Since its founding in 2007, the Norwin School District Community Foundation has helped facilitate more than $1.5 million in scholarship awards for Norwin High School students and more than $50,000 in Educator Innovation Grants. Those programs have grown through the support of donors who have chosen to invest in Norwin students and schools.

In a new video message, Foundation President Dr. Jack Boylan introduces this year's Days of Giving and explains how the campaign fits into the Foundation's broader work.

The video also begins a series of conversations the Foundation will share in the coming weeks. Educators, parents and students will discuss their own experiences with Foundation-supported programs and the effect those opportunities have had on their classrooms, families and plans for the future.

Donors may support a specific Foundation priority or allow their gift to be directed where it is most needed. [Make your gift today](https://nsdcf.org/giving).
