Norwin students are learning the building blocks of technology—one fold at a time! The “Origami Circuits” project, supported by a Norwin Educator Innovation Grant, introduces elementary students to the fundamentals of electricity and circuitry through hands-on, creative design. By blending art and science, this program sparks curiosity and helps young learners connect classroom concepts to real-world technology.

The Educator Innovation Grant program, funded by the Norwin School District Community Foundation (NSDCF), supports innovative classroom ideas that enhance and enrich student learning. Since 2010, more than $30,000 in grants have helped Norwin educators bring creativity and curiosity to life in the classroom.
